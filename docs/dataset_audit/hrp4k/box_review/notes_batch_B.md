- Sheets viewed: 91/91 (train_0092 to train_0182). None skipped. Tiles T01821 to T03640 (1820 tiles).

Judgement calls and caveats
- Unclear was used heavily (63%). Many boxes are tiny dark marks on concrete or pale patches at low resolution; they were not rejected on suspicion.
- `stain` (38 tiles, e.g. T02731-T02740, T03018-T03060 range, T03041-T03044) = tyre tracks in sand/dust on asphalt (no visible depression). Reason label is a stand-in; arguably "tracks".
- `drain` includes boxes on gutter grates and gutter strips beside kerbs with traffic cones (repeated site). Some of these boxes sit next to rather than on the grate; a few cone-site gutters were left unclear when no grate was visible.
- `manhole` includes round recessed ring/lid shapes (e.g. T02421-T02423, T03604) where I judged a cover frame rather than a hole; moderate confidence. Round pale/yellow oval items with no rim (e.g. T02048, T03081) were marked ok.
- `patch` = smooth, flush, pale repair patches (T03223-T03232 group, T02771, T03289). Moderate confidence.
- `crack` = long open/thin cracks with no visible missing material.
- Broken asphalt beside a manhole was marked ok only where the box clearly covered the broken area.
